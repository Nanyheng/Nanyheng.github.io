Open-Source Research Software

These MATLAB implementations accompany my work on adaptive-rank numerical methods for kinetic equations.

High-Dimensional SLAR

Adaptive-rank Vlasov–Poisson solvers for 2D2V and 3D3V configurations, corresponding to four- and six-dimensional phase spaces. The repository includes Hierarchical Tucker Adaptive Cross Approximation routines for sampling high-dimensional tensors without assembling every entry.

View repository →

LoMaC-SLAR

Locally macroscopic conservative semi-Lagrangian adaptive-rank solvers for Vlasov–Poisson simulations in 1D1V and 2D2V configurations. The method is designed to preserve mass, momentum, and energy.
