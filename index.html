About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Delaware, working with Prof. Jing-Mei Qiu. I received my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Xiamen University under the supervision of Prof. Jianxian Qiu.

My research lies in numerical analysis and scientific computing. I develop efficient, high-order, and structure-preserving numerical methods for kinetic equations and other transport-dominated partial differential equations.

Research Interests

  • Adaptive low-rank and tensor methods for high-dimensional kinetic equations
  • Structure-preserving semi-Lagrangian methods
  • High-order WENO and HWENO methods for hyperbolic conservation laws
  • Computational plasma physics and quantum kinetic models

Current Work

I am currently developing adaptive-rank solvers for high-dimensional Vlasov, Wigner–Poisson, and BGK systems, with an emphasis on reducing computational cost while preserving physically important quantities over long-time simulations.

Research Software

Open-source MATLAB implementations are available for high-dimensional SLAR and locally macroscopic conservative SLAR methods.
