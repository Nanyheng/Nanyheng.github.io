Research

My research focuses on efficient and reliable numerical simulation of kinetic equations and transport-dominated partial differential equations. The central challenge is to resolve high-dimensional phase-space dynamics without sacrificing the conservation and stability properties needed for physically credible long-time simulations.

Adaptive Low-Rank Tensor Methods

I develop sampling-based adaptive-rank algorithms that discover low-rank structure without assembling the full high-dimensional tensor. These methods combine semi-Lagrangian evolution with matrix and hierarchical tensor cross approximation, substantially reducing the storage and computational cost of four- and six-dimensional kinetic simulations.

Structure-Preserving Algorithms

My work also develops conservative non-splitting schemes for kinetic and fluid models. Local macroscopic conservative corrections are incorporated into adaptive-rank evolution to preserve mass, momentum, and energy while retaining high-order accuracy.

High-Order Transport Methods

I study Eulerian–Lagrangian WENO and Hermite WENO discretizations for nonlinear convection–diffusion equations, Vlasov–Poisson systems, and related transport problems. The goal is to combine large stable time steps, high-order accuracy, and robust treatment of complex solution structures.
